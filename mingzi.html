The Art of Restraint.

Sourced from multi-generational tanneries in Tuscany, our hides are drum-dyed and naturally tumbled to preserve the skin's living organic structure. Every piece evolves gracefully, developing a rich patina over decades of wear.

Eschewing industrial stamping, each metal hardware component begins as a hand-sculpted wax model, cast in solid brass, and polished manually to a liquid mirror finish.
